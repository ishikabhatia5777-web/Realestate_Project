# Residential / Villa / Apartment / Townhouse Appraisal — Sales Comparison Approach

Covers Aura Estate's residential, villa, apartment, and townhouse categories. The primary method is the **Sales Comparison Approach**: value is derived from what similar homes nearby have actually sold for. All four categories follow the same core steps below — differences are in which adjustment factors matter most (called out per category).

## Steps

1. **Define the subject property**: category (residential/villa/apartment/townhouse), built-up area, carpet area if known, number of bedrooms, floor (for apartments), age, condition, furnishing status, parking, amenities.

2. **Select comparables**: 3–5 properties, ideally:
   - Same neighborhood or a comparable one nearby
   - Same property type and similar size (within ~20%)
   - Sold or listed within the last 6–12 months
   - Similar age/condition

3. **Normalize to price per unit area** (e.g. price per sq ft) for each comparable — this makes properties of different sizes comparable.

4. **Adjust for differences**, typically as a % or flat amount per factor:
   - Location/micro-market desirability (e.g. proximity to transit, schools, main roads)
   - Floor level and view (apartments)
   - Age and condition (renovated vs original)
   - Amenities (clubhouse, pool, gym, security) for apartment complexes
   - Parking availability
   - Furnishing status

5. **Reconcile**: weight comparables more heavily if they need fewer/smaller adjustments (i.e. are more similar to the subject). Arrive at a price-per-unit-area estimate, then multiply by the subject's area for a total value range.

## Worked example (illustrative)

Subject: 1,200 sq ft apartment, 5 years old, 4th floor, unfurnished.

| Comparable | Area (sq ft) | Price | ₹/sq ft | Adjustments | Adjusted ₹/sq ft |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 1,150 | 63,25,000 | 5,500 | +2% (subject slightly larger) | 5,610 |
| B | 1,300 | 68,90,000 | 5,300 | -3% (comp is newer/higher floor) | 5,141 |
| C | 1,180 | 61,36,000 | 5,200 | +5% (comp needs renovation) | 5,460 |

Weighted estimate ≈ ₹5,400/sq ft → subject value ≈ ₹64.8 lakh, range ₹62–68 lakh depending on final condition/negotiation factors.

## Category-specific adjustment factors

- **Villa**: private land/plot size, pool, privacy/setback from neighbors, gated-community status, staff quarters, architectural distinction/brand of builder — these carry real weight in luxury villa pricing.
- **Apartment**: floor level and view, building/complex amenities (clubhouse, pool, gym, concierge, security), HOA/maintenance fees, elevator access.
- **Townhouse**: shared-wall vs. end-unit, private outdoor space (yard/terrace), HOA rules and fees, position within the row/complex.

## Notes

- For unique or high-end homes with few true comparables, note the reduced confidence explicitly — common for luxury villas and premium apartments.
- Rental yield (comparable rents × a market cap rate) can be used as a secondary sanity check, not the primary method, for residential.
